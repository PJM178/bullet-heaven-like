# Art style

The game is 3D with a **PlayStation 1-era look**. Use the era's techniques,
but keep every character and design original. References are for feel and
technique only, never something to copy:

- **PS1 era**: Final Fantasy VII (character style), Silent Hill
  (atmosphere, muted mood).
- **Modern PS1-style games**: Arctic Eggs, Crow Country (also a close
  reference for our high, angled camera), Signalis, Ground Zero (grounded,
  realistically proportioned characters in a muted setting).

- **Low poly.** Small triangle budgets with a visible, faceted silhouette.
  This is also what keeps hundreds of enemies cheap on mobile.
- **Simple colour.** Flat vertex colours. If textures are used, keep them
  tiny and pixel-sharp.
- **Grounded proportions.** Slim, roughly realistic human figures, not
  chibi or blocky caricatures.
- **Modular characters.** One shared base body and rig (a neutral grey
  mannequin), with clothing, hair and enemy variations built on top of it.
- **Determined motion.** Animation is purposeful and weighty, never comedic
  or overly exaggerated.
- **Choppy playback.** Poses update at a low rate (PS1-style stepped
  animation) while movement and the camera stay smooth.
