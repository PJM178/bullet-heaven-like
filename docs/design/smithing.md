# Equipment Smithing

Status: v0.2, 2026-11-14. Idea only: nothing is decided beyond wanting to
explore it; crafting materials are proposed. A living design document: it records what is decided, marks
proposals, and lists open questions.

## Overview

The idea: the player can **smith equipment**. That is all there is so far.
What equipment is, what smithing does to it, and where it sits in the game
are all open.

The game has no equipment today. The systems it would most likely touch are
the in-run skills in [weapon-plan.md](weapon-plan.md) and the coins and base
area in [meta-progression.md](meta-progression.md).

## Decided

Nothing yet.

## Proposed

- **Crafting materials.** Coins are the meta currency
  ([meta-progression.md](meta-progression.md)); smithing would probably
  bring in a second kind of thing to collect: crafting materials, used for
  smithing. Leaning yes, not settled. Which materials exist, where they
  come from and whether they are kept between runs are open.

## Open questions

Equipment:

- [ ] What counts as equipment: weapons, armour, accessories? How many
      slots does a character have?
- [ ] What does equipment change: plain stats, the skills on offer, how a
      skill behaves?
- [ ] How does equipment relate to the skills learned in a run
      ([weapon-plan.md](weapon-plan.md))? Is it a separate layer on top?
- [ ] Is equipment kept between runs, or found and lost within one?

Smithing:

- [ ] What does smithing do: make new equipment, upgrade existing pieces,
      combine pieces, or a mix?
- [ ] What does it cost: materials only, or materials and coins?
- [ ] Is the result certain, or is there chance involved (can it fail, are
      the stats rolled)?
- [ ] Where does it happen: in the base area between runs, at a point of
      interest during a run, or both?

Crafting materials:

- [ ] Which materials exist? Are there tiers or rarities, as with coins?
- [ ] Where do they come from: enemies, breakables, events
      ([maps-and-events.md](maps-and-events.md)), specific maps?
- [ ] Are they picked up like coins (by touch, see
      [pickups.md](pickups.md)) or awarded another way?
- [ ] Are they kept between runs like coins, and are all of them kept on
      death?
- [ ] Are they used only for smithing, or for other things too?
- [ ] Can coins and materials be exchanged for each other?

Fit with the rest:

- [ ] Is smithing the shape the meta progression's upgrades take (see
      Upgrades in [meta-progression.md](meta-progression.md)), or one
      system beside others?
